FLOORGUARD PROTOCOL
A plain-language explanation and a guide for use
This repository is an implementation, internal security audit, and test suite.
It is not an independent third-party audit, not a mainnet release, and not a
promise that every marketplace honors the floor.


WHAT IT IS

FloorGuard is an Ethereum protocol that refuses an NFT sale when the price
is below a recorded floor. The floor is denominated in one pinned Circle USDC
contract. In the founding launch reference example, the collection uses 69
ERC-721 tokens and an immutable floor of 100,000 USDC (100000000000 base
units because USDC uses 6 decimals). Creators and deployers can configure any
total supply (or uncapped) and any immutable founding floor upon deployment.

A sale is checked only on a path this protocol controls:

  - FloorGuardDirectMarket, the protocol's own atomic listing contract
  - a canonical Seaport 1.6 restricted order whose zone is FloorGuardSeaportZone

The contracts are not upgradeable. Nobody, including the administrator and
the timelock, can lower the founding floor. The direct market never holds
the buyer's USDC, and it has no withdraw function.


WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

These paths are not protected:

  - a vanilla opensea.io web listing or WETH bid, because opensea.io uses
    OpenSea's SignedZone rather than custom price-floor zones (transfers fail
    closed on FloorGuardNFT)
  - an unrestricted Seaport order, because Seaport does not call the zone
  - Magic Eden, because there is no adapter for it
  - a private wallet transfer that is an owner-initiated gift (operator == from),
    including an off-chain payment paired with an on-chain gift, wrapping into
    a vault, or an EIP-7702 delegated swap executed by the owner
  - a registered collection whose own NFT contract does not call
    FloorGuardTransferValidator, except when the sale itself goes through
    an authorized adapter

A gift is allowed. A sale below the floor, on a protected path, is not.


WHO CAN DO WHAT

Governance owner
  One address (migrable to a Safe via Ownable2Step; renounceOwnership is
  disabled). Can schedule, cancel, and execute matured changes after the
  timelock delay (48 hours minimum, 30 days maximum). Cannot skip the delay,
  pause immediately, lower the founding floor, move someone else's NFT, or
  take sale proceeds.

Guardian
  Separate address from the governance owner. Can immediately pause the direct
  market, the Seaport zone, or both, and can call emergencyZeroFee() to zero
  the direct-market fee if feeRecipient is ever USDC-blacklisted. Cannot
  unpause, raise fees, change a floor, transfer an NFT, or approve an adapter.
  Unpausing waits for the timelock.

Collection administrator
  For the founding collection, the administrator is FloorGuardTimelock.
  For a third-party collection, the collection admin can raise that
  collection's floor immediately when the policy allows it, set a higher
  per-token floor (subject to the collection's policy), and opt into adapters
  the timelock has already approved. Cannot change another collection, and
  cannot lower the founding floor.

NFT owner of the founding collection
  Can mint up to the configured maxSupply (69 in the founding reference
  example), set metadata, and set an ERC-2981 royalty of at most 10 percent.
  Cannot raise maxSupply, burn a token, or take a token back after it has
  been transferred.

Sensitive changes wait for the timelock (48 hours to 30 days). Those include
raising the founding floor, lowering a GovernanceAdjustable floor, changing
the market fee, approving or removing an adapter, upgrading the Seaport zone,
replacing the guardian, and unpausing sales.
A guardian pause or emergencyZeroFee is immediate. Cancelling your own listing
or calling invalidateAllListings() is immediate, including while sales are
paused.


PRICE POLICIES

NonDecreasing
  The floor can go up. It cannot go down (on either the collection floor or
  per-token overrides). The founding collection uses this policy and starts at
  the configured foundingFloor (100,000 USDC in the launch reference example).

Fixed
  The floor is the value recorded at registration. Neither collection floors
  nor per-token overrides can move.

GovernanceAdjustable
  The timelock can raise or lower the floor (and token overrides) after the
  timelock delay. A collection administrator can raise it immediately, but
  cannot lower it.

A per-token floor, when one is set, is the price that must be met if it
is above the collection floor.

Read the price that a sale must meet:

  effectiveFloor(collection, tokenId)

That value is in USDC base units. Divide by 1,000,000 to read it in USDC.
In the launch reference example, 100,000 USDC is 100000000000.


HOW A SELLER USES THE DIRECT MARKET

1. Confirm the collection is registered and that FloorGuardDirectMarket
   is an authorized sale path for it. isSalePathAuthorized(collection,
   market) must be true.

2. Read the floor:

     effectiveFloor(collection, tokenId)

   The listing price must be greater than or equal to that number.

3. Approve the market contract to transfer that NFT. list() does not pull
   the token. buy() transfers it from you, so the approval has to be in
   place before a buyer can complete the sale.

4. Call:

     list(collection, tokenId, price, expiry)
     (or list(collection, tokenId, price, expiry, minSellerProceeds))

   You must own the token. expiry is a unix timestamp in the future.
   price is USDC base units. minSellerProceeds protects your net payout if
   royalties or fees change before a buyer fills the listing. One token per
   listing. There are no bundles and no partial fills. Re-listing the same
   token automatically supersedes your prior listing.

5. To take a listing down, call cancel(listingId), or invalidateAllListings()
   to invalidate all prior listings in one call. Both work while paused.


HOW A BUYER USES THE DIRECT MARKET

1. Read the listing. It must still be active, unexpired (block.timestamp <
   expiry), match the seller's active nonce, and be priced at or above the
   current effective floor. If the floor was raised after the listing was
   created, a stale underpriced listing cannot be bought.

2. Approve the pinned USDC contract so the market can pull the full price
   from your wallet. The market pays the seller, the royalty receiver, and
   any protocol fee directly. It does not custody the payment.

3. Call buy(listingId) from a wallet that is not the seller.

   Following Checks-Effects-Interactions, the market pulls USDC first and
   then transfers the NFT in the same transaction. If payment or transfer
   fails, the entire purchase reverts atomically.

A royalty comes from ERC-2981 on the collection (capped at 10%). The protocol
fee, if one has been set, is also capped at 10%. Royalty plus fee cannot
exceed the price.


HOW A SEAPORT 1.6 ORDER USES THE FLOOR

This is a separate path from the direct market. It applies only when all
of the following are true:

  - the order is a canonical Seaport 1.6 restricted order (FULL_RESTRICTED
    or PARTIAL_RESTRICTED) with zoneHash == 0 and offerer != fulfiller
  - the zone is FloorGuardSeaportZone
  - Seaport is the pinned deployment
    0x0000000000000068F116a894984e2DB1123eB395
  - the order is either a single-NFT ask (1 ERC-721 offered for pinned USDC)
    or a single-NFT buyer bid (pinned USDC offered for 1 ERC-721)
  - every payment item is pinned Circle USDC paid to qualified recipients
    (seller + capped ERC-2981 royalty + capped allowlisted fee recipients;
    fulfiller tips and address(0) recipients are rejected)
  - the qualified USDC amounts add up to at least the effective floor
  - startTime <= block.timestamp < endTime (endTime is exclusive)
  - the collection has opted into this zone

For the founding collection, the transfer operator must be that pinned Seaport
contract (when conduitKey == 0) or the OpenSea conduit
0x1E0049783F008A0085193E00003D00cd54003c71. Buyers may deliver the paid NFT to
any non-zero recipient (such as a cold wallet or Safe).

An ordinary opensea.io web listing does not attach this zone because opensea.io
uses OpenSea's SignedZone. Vanilla opensea.io listings fail closed.


HOW ANOTHER COLLECTION JOINS

A collection with an owner() can register itself through the registry,
choosing a policy and a starting floor above zero. The registrant becomes
that collection's administrator.

Registration records a floor. It does not, by itself, make the collection's
own transfer function refuse a cheap sale. Full transfer protection exists
for the founding NFT because that contract calls the transfer validator.
Another collection is protected when the sale goes through an authorized
adapter. Its own contract protects transfers only if it calls
FloorGuardTransferValidator.

The collection administrator can hand the role to a new address in two
steps: proposeAdmin, then acceptAdmin from the new address (or cancelProposedAdmin
to revoke a pending handoff).

New marketplace adapters are separate contracts. The timelock must approve
one before a collection can opt into it. There is no Magic Eden adapter
in this repository.


PAUSING

The guardian can pause the direct market, the Seaport zone, or both (and can
immediately zero direct-market fees via emergencyZeroFee). Paused sales cannot
be opened or completed. Sellers can still cancel or bulk-invalidate listings.
Unpausing (unpauseDirectMarket, unpauseSeaportZone, unpauseAll) is a timelock
action and waits 48 hours to 30 days.

If the administrator key is lost, pause, emergencyZeroFee, gifts, and sales
that are already authorized keep their existing rules. Sensitive changes stop,
because there is no backup signer until ownership of FloorGuardGovernance is
accepted by a Safe.


WHERE THIS RUNS

Sepolia first. Mainnet only after the gates in DEPLOYMENT.md and
SECURITY_MODEL.md.

  Ethereum mainnet USDC   0xA0b86991c6218b36c1d19D4a2e9Eb0cE3606eB48

This repository does not publish a FloorGuard deployment address. Deploy
with script/Deploy.s.sol from a keystore or a hardware wallet. The script
reads FLOORGUARD_* addresses and deployment settings from the environment. It
does not read a private key.

Build and test:

  forge install
  forge test
