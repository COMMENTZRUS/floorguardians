FLOORGUARD PROTOCOL
A plain-language explanation and a guide for use
This repository is an implementation and a test suite. It is not an audit,
not a mainnet release, and not a promise that every marketplace honors the floor.


WHAT IT IS

FloorGuard is an Ethereum protocol that refuses an NFT sale when the price
is below a recorded floor. The floor is denominated in one pinned USDC
contract. The founding collection is 69 ERC-721 tokens. Each of those tokens
has a floor of 100,000 USDC, which is 100000000000 base units because USDC
uses 6 decimals.

A sale is checked only on a path this protocol controls:

  - FloorGuardDirectMarket, the protocol's own listing contract
  - a Seaport 1.6 restricted order whose zone is FloorGuardSeaportZone

The contracts are not upgradeable. Nobody, including the administrator and
the timelock, can lower the founding floor. The direct market never holds
the buyer's USDC, and it has no withdraw function.


WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

These paths are not protected:

  - an OpenSea or other listing that does not use this Seaport zone
  - an unrestricted Seaport order, because Seaport does not call the zone
  - Magic Eden, because there is no adapter for it
  - a private wallet transfer that is an owner-initiated gift
  - an off-chain payment paired with an on-chain gift
  - a registered collection whose own NFT contract does not call
    FloorGuardTransferValidator, except when the sale itself goes through
    an authorized adapter

A gift is allowed. A sale below the floor, on a protected path, is not.


WHO CAN DO WHAT

Governance owner
  One address. Can schedule, cancel, and execute changes after a 48-hour
  timelock. Can later hand this role to a Safe. Cannot skip the delay,
  lower the founding floor, move someone else's NFT, or take sale proceeds.

Guardian
  Can pause the direct market, the Seaport zone, or both. Cannot unpause,
  change a floor, transfer an NFT, or approve an adapter. Unpausing waits
  for the timelock.

Collection administrator
  For a collection that is not the founding collection, can raise that
  collection's floor immediately when the policy allows it, set a higher
  per-token floor, and opt into adapters the timelock has already approved.
  Cannot change another collection, and cannot lower the founding floor.

NFT owner of the founding collection
  Can mint until 69 tokens exist, set metadata, and set a royalty of at
  most 10 percent. Cannot raise the supply, burn a token, or take a token
  back after it has been transferred.

Sensitive changes wait 48 hours. Those include raising the founding floor,
lowering a GovernanceAdjustable floor, changing the market fee, approving
or removing an adapter, replacing the guardian, and unpausing sales.
A guardian pause is immediate. Cancelling your own listing is immediate,
including while sales are paused.


PRICE POLICIES

NonDecreasing
  The floor can go up. It cannot go down. The founding collection uses
  this policy and starts at 100,000 USDC.

Fixed
  The floor is the value recorded at registration. It does not move.

GovernanceAdjustable
  The timelock can raise or lower the floor after 48 hours. A collection
  administrator can still raise it immediately, but cannot lower it.

A per-token floor, when one is set, is the price that must be met if it
is above the collection floor. For the founding collection, a token floor
cannot be decreased either.

Read the price that a sale must meet:

  effectiveFloor(collection, tokenId)

That value is in USDC base units. Divide by 1,000,000 to read it in USDC.
100,000 USDC is 100000000000.


HOW A SELLER USES THE DIRECT MARKET

1. Confirm the collection is registered and that FloorGuardDirectMarket
   is an authorized sale path for it. isSalePathAuthorized(collection,
   market) must be true.

2. Read the floor:

     effectiveFloor(collection, tokenId)

   The listing price must be greater than or equal to that number.

3. Approve the market contract to transfer that NFT. list() does not pull
   the token. buy() transfers it from you, so the approval has to be in
   place before a buyer can complete the sale.

4. Call:

     list(collection, tokenId, price, expiry)

   You must own the token. expiry is a unix timestamp in the future.
   price is USDC base units. One token per listing. There are no bundles
   and no partial fills.

5. To take it down, call cancel(listingId). You can cancel while the
   market is paused.


HOW A BUYER USES THE DIRECT MARKET

1. Read the listing. It must still be active, unexpired, and priced at
   or above the current effective floor. If the floor was raised after
   the listing was created, a stale underpriced listing cannot be bought.

2. Approve the pinned USDC contract so the market can pull the full price
   from your wallet. The market pays the seller, the royalty receiver, and
   any protocol fee directly. It does not custody the payment.

3. Call buy(listingId) from a wallet that is not the seller.

   The market transfers the NFT to you and pulls USDC in the same
   transaction. If the NFT does not arrive, or the USDC received is short,
   the purchase reverts.

A royalty comes from ERC-2981 on the collection. The protocol fee, if one
has been set, is at most the cap enforced by the market. Royalty plus fee
cannot exceed the price.


HOW A SEAPORT ORDER USES THE FLOOR

This is a separate path from the direct market. It applies only when all
of the following are true:

  - the order is a Seaport 1.6 restricted order
  - the zone is FloorGuardSeaportZone
  - Seaport is the pinned deployment
    0x0000000000000068F116a894984e2DB1123eB395
  - the offer is one ERC-721, amount 1
  - every consideration item is the pinned USDC
  - the USDC amounts add up to at least the effective floor
  - the order's start and end times include the current block
  - the collection has opted into this zone

For the founding collection, the transfer operator must be that Seaport
contract or the OpenSea conduit
0x1E0049783F008A0085193E00003D00cd54003c71.

An ordinary OpenSea listing does not do this by itself. Current OpenSea
product behavior has to be checked against OpenSea's own documentation
before anyone treats a public listing as floor-protected.


HOW ANOTHER COLLECTION JOINS

A collection with an owner() can register itself through the registry,
choosing a policy and a starting floor above zero. The registrant becomes
that collection's administrator.

Registration records a floor. It does not, by itself, make the collection's
own transfer function refuse a cheap sale. Full transfer protection exists
for the founding NFT because that contract calls the transfer validator.
Another collection is protected when the sale goes through an authorized
adapter. Its own contract protects transfers only if it calls
FloorGuardTransferValidator.

The collection administrator can hand the role to a new address in two
steps: proposeAdmin, then acceptAdmin from the new address.

New marketplace adapters are separate contracts. The timelock must approve
one before a collection can opt into it. There is no Magic Eden adapter
in this repository.


PAUSING

The guardian can pause the direct market, the Seaport zone, or both.
Paused sales cannot be opened or completed. Sellers can still cancel a
direct-market listing. Unpausing is a timelock action and waits 48 hours.

If the administrator key is lost, pause, gifts, and sales that are already
authorized keep their existing rules. Sensitive changes stop, because there
is no backup signer until ownership of FloorGuardGovernance is accepted
by a Safe.


WHERE THIS RUNS

Sepolia first. Mainnet only after the gates in DEPLOYMENT.md.

  Ethereum mainnet USDC   0xA0b86991c6218b36c1d19D4a2e9Eb0cE3606eB48

This repository does not publish a FloorGuard deployment address. Deploy
with script/Deploy.s.sol from a keystore or a hardware wallet. The script
reads the administrator and guardian addresses from the environment. It
does not read a private key.

Build and test:

  forge install
  forge test
